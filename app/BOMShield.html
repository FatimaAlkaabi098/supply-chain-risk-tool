BOMShield scores an AI server BOM for supply-chain risk before purchase and returns a procurement decision: approve, approve with conditions, or reject.

Your file is read entirely in this browser. Nothing is uploaded, stored or sent anywhere.

Required columns

component_id, component_name, category, vendor, model, version, country_of_origin, quantity, end_of_life, lead_time_weeks, validated_alternative

What you get

  • Five risk dimensions per component, each explained
  • CVE evidence with CVSS, KEV and EPSS
  • Components that cannot be identified, surfaced not hidden
  • A what-if simulator showing the smallest set of changes that makes the purchase acceptable
